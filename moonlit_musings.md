# Personal thoughts, theories, and reflections on space/astrophysics.
This is the first official issue of my SkyEvents newsletter! I wanted to take some time to share 
my thoughts on the journey of building this application.

It’s been about a year since I started this project for myself. I’m a space enthusiast—hence the 
name SpaceJunkie, and I thought it would be cool to have something simple and functional that tells 
me what’s happening in the sky.

I didn’t want a lot of bells and whistles. I wanted the what, when, and where of sky events, so I 
could grab my telescope or binoculars, or simply use my own eyes, and look up at the wonders overhead.

And I wanted to share that wonder with others.

So instead of looking down at your phone, look up. You might find something amazing happening.
